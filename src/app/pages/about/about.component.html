I live for passion

From a curious teenager with a camera to a working photographer.

I was always drawn to photography. Right after high school I bought a semi-professional camera and started photographing everything in sight. Before long I fell for portraiture — studying light in detail and building the skills to work professionally.

Today my expertise lies in working with clothing brands, jewellery houses and crafting films. I have helped more than 30 brands represent themselves by delivering the quality they deserve — and I am just getting started.
